cozulmemisdosya — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the cozulmemisdosya application ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is a personal content-automation tool that publishes original, fictional short videos to the accounts owned by its operator.

1. Information we access

To publish videos to the operator's own connected accounts, the app uses access tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta) through their official OAuth authorization flows. These tokens allow the app to upload video content to the owner's own account. The app does not request or access other users' personal data.

2. How we use it

  • Access tokens are used solely to upload the operator's own finished videos to the operator's own accounts.
  • We do not read, collect, store, or repost other users' content.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage & security

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are used only to call the official platform APIs over secure connections. They are not transmitted to any server other than the official TikTok, Google and Meta APIs.

4. Data retention & deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use and can be revoked at any time by the operator from their TikTok, Google or Meta account security settings, which immediately ends the app's access.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly process any data from children under 13.
